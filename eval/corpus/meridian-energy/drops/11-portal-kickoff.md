# Meeting notes — Customer portal kickoff, 20 Jan 2025

Attendees: Aisha Bell (Head of Customer Experience, Meridian — first time
we've worked with her), Tom Nagel, our side: JN, new colleague RM shadowing.

A second engagement begins. **The Customer Portal project kicked off today** —
a self-service web portal for Meridian's energy customers. Aisha owns it on
the business side and has been pushing for it for two years; she came with
mock-ups, customer research, and a board deck. She is unambiguous about her
position: "this is the thing I came to Meridian to build." High energy,
completely bought in, and clearly influential with the CX organisation —
though she noted she's "spent two years being told no", so her clout at exec
level is still being established.

Scope agreed in the room for portal phase one, both confirmed by Aisha with
Tom nodding:

1. **Self-service account management** (contact details, payment method,
   moving house) — in.
2. **A usage dashboard** (energy consumption over time) — in.

Aisha wanted live chat in phase one as well; Tom pushed it to "phase two
discussion, not now" and Aisha accepted — explicitly not decided either way,
just not part of today's scope.

Friction, and it's structural: **both the portal and the billing replatform
need the same shared platform-API team for integration work, and there is not
enough of that team to go around.** Aisha wants them primarily on portal
("customer-facing wins the tie-break, surely"); Tom wants them protecting the
billing migration ("the replatform is the company's spine — the portal queues
behind it"). They went three rounds, perfectly civil, zero convergence. **Open
conflict between Aisha and Tom over shared platform-team capacity, affecting
both projects; unresolved.** JN to track — this will bite the moment
integration work starts.

Aisha's team demoed a competitor's portal for inspiration (a water utility);
colour only.
