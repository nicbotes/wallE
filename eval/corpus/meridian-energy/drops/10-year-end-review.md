# Meeting notes — Year-end programme review, 5 Dec 2024

Attendees: Tom Nagel (chair), Marcus Webb, Dana Okafor, our side: JN, KT.

Tom's verdict meeting — he'd promised his view of the replatform by year end.

**Verdict: the programme continues, and moves formally from the build phase
into the migration phase as of today.** Tom, chairing: "Build is done enough.
From today this is a migration programme — the only thing that matters now is
getting off Hermes safely." KT confirmed the phase gate criteria were met.
(For the record: this is a phase change for the replatform project, not a new
decision about its scope or architecture.)

Tom then said something worth capturing precisely, because it explains
everything he does. Asked by Marcus what success looks like for him
personally, Tom said: **"I'm interim. I get judged on quick wins, visibly
landed, before the permanent hire is chosen. That's what I'm optimising for —
give me things I can point at."** Stated in front of the whole room, no
reading-between-lines required.

Re-confirmations, no changes: Dana's dual-run requirement (Tom: "the dual-run
stays, it's the safest quick win there is"); scope exactly as it stands after
the October cuts; the July managed-cloud decision unchallenged ("cloud was
right", Tom).

The cutover conflict (Dana's zero-downtime weekend vs Marcus's no-overtime)
was raised by KT and immediately deferred again by mutual agreement to the new
year. Still open, still unresolved, nobody moved.
