# Workshop notes — Cutover planning II, 10 Sep 2025

Attendees: Dana Okafor, Marcus Webb, Tom Nagel (facilitating), our side: KT.

Sixteen months after the first cutover workshop ended in deadlock, Tom got
Dana and Marcus in a room with a proposal his team had costed over the summer.

**The proposal, and the decision: a phased cutover.** Customer accounts
migrate from Hermes to the new platform in six tranches over six consecutive
month-ends, riding the existing month-end operational windows. Each tranche
is small enough that Dana's existing on-shift team covers it inside normal
working patterns — **no weekend big-bang, therefore no overtime budget** —
and billing service stays up throughout because Hermes keeps running the
not-yet-migrated tranches — **therefore zero downtime for operations**.

**Decided in the meeting, jointly by Dana and Marcus** (Tom explicitly made
them co-owners of the decision: "you two sign it or we don't do it"):
**the cutover will be phased, per the six-tranche plan.** Marcus: "no
overtime line, so I'm signed." Dana: "billing never goes dark and I verify
every tranche against the dual-run — signed."

**This resolves the cutover conflict that has been open between Dana and
Marcus since May 2024.** Both said so in terms; KT read the original logged
positions back and both confirmed the matter is closed by this decision.

One consequence for the requirement record: Dana's zero-downtime-weekend-
cutover requirement as originally stated (the specific weekend/overtime
mechanism) is **superseded by the phased approach** — the intent (billing
never goes dark) is fully met by the phased plan, but the requirement as
written no longer describes what will happen. Dana agreed to record it as
superseded rather than delivered "as long as nobody pretends I changed my
mind about downtime."

Tranche one targets the November month-end. KT to publish the tranche plan.

Biscuits were superior this time (Marcus brought them, unexpensed — noted
without comment).
