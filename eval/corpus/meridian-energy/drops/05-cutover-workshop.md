# Workshop notes — Cutover planning, 8 May 2024

Attendees: Dana Okafor, Marcus Webb, Priya Sharma (remote, patchy audio),
our side: JN, KT.

Purpose: how the eventual switch from Hermes to the new platform actually
happens. It got tense.

Dana's position, stated as a hard requirement: **the cutover must be
zero-downtime for billing operations** — her 41 staff cannot have the billing
system unavailable during a working day, so any switch has to happen over a
weekend with full overtime cover from her team and engineering. "Must", again
her word, and she said she'd escalate if it's watered down.

Marcus's position, equally hard: **he will not approve weekend overtime budget
for a cutover**. "We are not paying double-time for a hundred people because
engineering can't do it during business hours." He suggested the system could
tolerate a planned outage window instead. Dana visibly bristled.

**These two positions are directly contradictory and the meeting ended without
any resolution — Dana wants a zero-downtime weekend cutover requiring overtime;
Marcus refuses the overtime spend.** Priya tried to broker on the call but the
audio kept dropping and she gave up, asking us to log it and move on. This is
now the second live conflict on the programme.

Colour: Marcus told a long story about a disastrous ERP cutover at his previous
company (a retailer) — background to his position, no new facts about Meridian.

No decisions were made in this meeting. To be explicit: the cutover approach
itself remains completely open.

Actions: JN to log the conflict; revisit when migration phase is closer.
