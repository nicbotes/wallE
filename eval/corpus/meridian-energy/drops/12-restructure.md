# Email — Meridian internal announcement, 11 Mar 2025

From: Office of the CEO, Meridian Energy
Subject: Technology leadership and organisation changes
(Forwarded to us by Tom Nagel: "FYI — official as of today.")

Colleagues,

Following the conclusion of our executive search, we are delighted to announce
that **Tom Nagel has been appointed Chief Technology Officer on a permanent
basis**, effective immediately. The board thanks Tom for his impact as interim
and looks forward to his leadership of the technology strategy.

As part of the same review, the **Billing Operations function moves from the
Finance division into the Technology division**. Dana Okafor continues as Head
of Billing Operations and **now reports to Tom Nagel** (previously the interim
arrangement; now permanent, and her function sits in Technology rather than
Finance).

There are no other reporting changes. [Sections on a facilities move in the
Leeds office and the new travel-expense policy omitted — nothing relevant.]

---

Our note (JN): rang Tom to congratulate him. Two things worth the record.
One — his stance on the replatform has visibly turned: his words, "now that
it's mine to own rather than audit, I'm backing it properly; the migration
plan is sound and I'll say so at the board." That's the interim scepticism
gone; he described himself as "a supporter, not yet a cheerleader". Two — with
the permanent role secured, his stated 'quick wins' motivation from December
is spent; he says his horizon is now "the three-year technology strategy".
