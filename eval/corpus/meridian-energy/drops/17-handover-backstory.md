# Meeting notes — Handover / history session, 14 Jan 2026

Attendees: Dana Okafor, Marcus Webb, our side: JN, RM (taking over the account
from JN). Purpose: JN is rotating off; RM needs the backstory that isn't in any
document. Dana and Marcus did most of the talking, about the past.

Almost nothing here is news about *now* — it is history we never had.

**The 2021 CRM programme.** Marcus explained something none of us knew: before
our engagement, Meridian ran an internal CRM replacement that failed badly. It
was cancelled in November 2021 after eighteen months and about £4m, with
nothing shipped. Marcus: "That's the ghost in every room you've been in. When I
said 'prove me wrong' at your kickoff, I was talking about that programme, not
about you." So his cost scepticism has a specific origin, and it dates from
2021, not from us.

**Dana's real starting position.** Dana added — and this reframes her whole
first year with us — that she was the operational lead on that 2021 CRM
programme and personally carried the fallout when it collapsed. "I wasn't
neutral at your kickoff. I'd decided long before you walked in that I'd believe
the new system when I saw it run, and not before." Worth recording: her stated
position in January 2024 was wait-and-see, but the reason for it predates us by
two years.

**A governance decision we've been complying with without knowing it existed.**
Marcus mentioned in passing that after the 2021 failure the board adopted a
standing rule — no vendor gets access to production customer data without a
completed security review, decided by the board on 3 February 2022 and still in
force. Marcus was the one who took it to the board. Nobody ever told us; our
security review in early 2024 happened because their procurement team asked for
it, and now we know why. Still active, never superseded.

**One correction to our own record, offered unprompted.** Dana said the CSV
export requirement from the January 2024 requirements workshop — the one we
logged with nobody's name on it — was hers. She raised it on behalf of the
audit team and then forgot she'd raised it. "Put my name on it, it's mine."

Housekeeping: RM added to the standing invites, JN off the account from
February. Leaving drinks in the usual place. Marcus asked whether the coffee
machine on their floor had ever been fixed; it has not.
