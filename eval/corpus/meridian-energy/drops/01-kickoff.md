# Meeting notes — Billing replatform kickoff, 10 Jan 2024

Attendees: Priya Sharma (VP Engineering, Meridian), Marcus Webb (CFO, Meridian),
Dana Okafor (Head of Billing Operations, Meridian), our side: JN, KT.

Ran long — 90 min. Coffee machine on their floor was broken, which Marcus joked
was "the first budget cut of the year".

Priya opened: the legacy billing system ("Hermes", in-house, 14 years old) is
the biggest drag on the engineering roadmap and she wants it replaced. She was
emphatic: "I will personally clear roadblocks for this — it's my top priority
for the year." She's driving the programme and has pre-briefed the exec team.

Marcus was blunt that he's not yet convinced: "Every replatform I've seen has
doubled its budget. Prove me wrong." He said his sole test for the programme is
total cost — licence, delivery, and run-rate — and that he'll support it only
while the numbers hold. Priya and Marcus both confirmed the project is approved
to start regardless: kickoff of the **Billing Replatform** project is official
as of today, discovery phase first.

Dana walked through billing ops today: 41 staff, month-end is "controlled
chaos", every billing error lands on her team's desk. She didn't take a
position for or against the replatform — "show me it works and I'm happy" —
but wants to be consulted on anything that touches operational process.

Scope discussion (Priya at the whiteboard). Agreed as in scope for the
replatform, all three confirmed by Priya in the wrap-up:

1. Replace the Hermes billing engine itself (rating + invoicing runs).
2. Invoice generation and delivery (PDF + email pipeline).
3. Migration of the customer billing history out of Hermes.

Marcus asked whether the finance data warehouse should be modernised at the
same time; Priya said that is explicitly a separate conversation for another
year — parked, not part of this programme. Nobody disagreed.

Reporting lines mentioned in passing: Priya and Marcus both sit on the exec;
Dana reports to Priya. (JN to confirm the org chart.)

Next: requirements workshop with Dana's leads, two weeks out.
