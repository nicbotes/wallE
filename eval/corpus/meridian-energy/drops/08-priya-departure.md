# Email — forwarded internal announcement, received 15 Sep 2024

From: Priya Sharma
To: Technology division, all
Subject: Moving on — and introducing Tom Nagel
Forwarded to us by Priya with a one-line note: "Wanted you to hear it from me
first. Look after the replatform."

Team,

After six brilliant years at Meridian, I'm leaving at the end of this month to
take a group CTO role elsewhere. My last day is 27 September.

The billing replatform remains, in my view, the most important thing this
division is doing. Please give it the support you've given me.

From 1 October, **Tom Nagel joins as interim CTO**, covering my
responsibilities while the exec runs a permanent search. Tom comes from the
utilities sector and has led two major platform consolidations. He'll be based
in the Leeds office.

Please join me in welcoming Tom.

— Priya

---

Our note for the record (JN): spoke to Tom Nagel by phone the same afternoon.
First impressions: sharp, direct, and — his words — "instinctively suspicious
of big-bang replatforms; I've killed two in my career and I'll want this one
to earn its keep." He asked for the full cost history and said he'd form his
own view by year end. So: the programme's biggest champion has left, and her
interim replacement arrives openly sceptical of the whole endeavour. Dana and
the billing leads now report to Tom on the interim org chart (previously
Priya). Farewell drinks for Priya are the 26th; gift collection going round.
