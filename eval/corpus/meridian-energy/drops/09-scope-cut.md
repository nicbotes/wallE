# Meeting notes — Scope review ("the cut meeting"), 22 Oct 2024

Attendees: Marcus Webb (chair), Tom Nagel, Dana Okafor, our side: JN, KT.

Marcus called this one. Q3 numbers across Meridian are poor and every
programme is being asked to shed weight. He was direct: "I'm not killing the
replatform. I am shrinking it."

Two cuts were made, both by Marcus as chair, with Tom concurring:

1. **Mobile payments is now out of scope for the replatform — decided, not
   parked.** It has sat undecided since January; Marcus ruled it out today:
   "if the call centre wants it, they can fund it as their own project in
   two years." Dana didn't object — her team never owned it anyway.

2. **The API-first requirement is dropped.** Marcus: "APIs for teams that
   might want them someday is exactly the kind of gold-plating I'm cutting."
   Tom agreed — "ship the boring thing first." We noted for the record this
   was Priya's requirement; with her gone, nobody in the room defended it.
   Formally: requirement dropped, not delivered.

Dana used the opening to re-state — unprompted, verbatim — "dual-run for
three cycles survives every cut, or I escalate to the board." Marcus waved it
through: "your dual-run is safe, it's cheap insurance." No change, but a
re-confirmation under pressure worth noting.

Tom mostly listened. He repeated his line about the programme "earning its
keep" and said he'd give his verdict at the year-end review. He asked KT for
a one-page cost-to-complete, due November.

Nothing else changed: engine replacement, invoice generation, history
migration, and the Hermes stabilisation work all remain in scope. The cutover
conflict between Dana and Marcus remains open and was pointedly not discussed
("December problem", Marcus).

Ended on time, which with Marcus chairing is its own kind of message.
